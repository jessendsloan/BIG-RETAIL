# Big Retail — Brand Design

**Status:** Accepted opening foundation

## Purpose

Brands give the product world recognizable commercial identity. They are not supplier records and they are not product mechanics by themselves.

A brand answers:

> Who is this product from, and what kind of consumer identity does that name carry?

The goal is to make shelves feel like a believable retail world rather than a collection of anonymous SKU numbers.

## Core rules

- Brands and Suppliers are separate concepts.
- A brand may appear across multiple Product Lines.
- A supplier may carry products from many brands.
- Multiple suppliers may offer the same branded SKU.
- Brand identity should be visually recognizable and reusable across related SKUs.
- Brand names should sound commercially plausible first and funny second.
- Do not create a completely unrelated joke brand for every SKU.
- A brand may tend toward a market position, but Market Position belongs to the SKU and is not forced globally by the brand.

## Brand character rule

Brands are the recurring characters of the shelf world.

A player should eventually be able to recognize a brand family before reading every SKU name. Packaging, logo treatment, typography, and color language should reinforce that identity.

## Current scope

Only the opening brand identities above are active.

The read-only Commercial Directory presents these Brands with their identity, optional logo, and opening Products. This is a reference surface only; it does not add brand progression, loyalty, negotiations, or management mechanics.

Do not create full brand catalogs yet. Additional SKUs should be added when the Product Line / department design calls for them.
